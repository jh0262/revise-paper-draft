# Revise Paper Draft

Revise a manuscript as an evidence-bearing scientific argument, not merely polished prose. Preserve the author's technical meaning, notation, numerical values, intended contribution, and requested scope.

## Select mode and scope

Infer the mode from the request. Ask only when a missing choice materially changes the deliverable.

- **Diagnostic:** identify problems and prescribe fixes without rewriting the whole text.
- **Targeted revision:** revise only the requested paragraph, section, caption, or response.
- **Full-draft revision:** diagnose globally, then revise section by section.
- **Pre-submission audit:** check consistency, evidence boundaries, reproducibility, figures, citations, and verified venue requirements.
- **Reviewer response:** activate only when requested; permit respectful, evidence-based disagreement.

Use the requested output language. Distinguish algorithm-mechanism, problem-modelling/application, equipment/control, and thesis manuscripts. Do not turn an engineering application into a claim of a new general optimizer, or impose thesis-length background on a journal article.

When editing a document file, also use the applicable document/PDF workflow. Preserve formatting, editable equations, and tracked changes when requested.

## Establish the evidence boundary

Identify the research question, manuscript type, target venue if supplied, completed work, available evidence, and requested depth. Separate observations, definitions, design intentions, inferences, formal results, and unsupported statements.

Never invent experiments, data, parameter values, statistical significance, citations, DOIs, software settings, novelty, or validation. Do not convert planned work into completed work. Reference papers used for style are not evidence that the current manuscript has the same methods, results, or novelty.

Keep source observations, editorial recommendations, and verified venue requirements distinct. Protect formulas, operators, thresholds, model assumptions, values, units, and citations from silent substantive edits. When text, formula, figure, or code conflicts, record the locations and request the missing evidence. Correct an established error transparently; do not guess which version is intended.

## Apply the workflow

### 1. Form the central claim

Express the main contribution in one testable sentence with boundary conditions. Identify whether it concerns a mechanism, mathematical model, application adaptation, or engineered system. Ambiguity is an issue to resolve, not permission to invent novelty.

### 2. Align problems, mechanisms, and evidence

Connect each major claim through:

`condition/phase -> specific deficiency -> technical action -> intended mechanism -> evidence -> permitted conclusion -> limitation`

Record supporting figures, tables, equations, experiments, simulations, or citations; comparator; metric and direction; budget; conditions; evidence location; and status as relevant. A local rewrite may use this check without displaying a matrix. Use the worksheet when a full revision benefits from traceability.

### 3. Repair the logical skeleton

Check the dependency:

`problem -> gap -> research question -> method -> evidence -> interpretation -> boundary -> contribution`

Every Introduction promise should be answered later. Every conclusion should be supported earlier. Group literature by mechanisms or assumptions, rather than merely listing authors. Preserve sound organization and restructure only within the requested scope.

### 4. Revise by scientific function

For Chinese algorithm papers, use concrete technical verbs, stable terminology, explicit conditions, and bounded results. Explain both the operation and its rationale. Distinguish inherited components from proposed changes. Do not impose fixed paragraph counts, three innovations, particular test suites, 30 runs, or six baselines.

Method paragraphs connect motivation, definition, symbols, execution, and rationale. Results paragraphs separate observed differences from possible explanations. Preserve costs, unfavorable results, and exceptions that change the main claim. An illustrative example is not a completed experiment or a general proof.

### 5. Rewrite at the requested scope

Lead with revised text for direct rewrite requests. Preserve symbols, units, equation numbering, values, and citation placeholders. Replace vague praise only with information actually available. Distinguish correlation, prediction, explanation, and causation; do not inflate novelty.

Place safe revised prose separately from author queries and proposed research. Working drafts may contain explicit unresolved slots, but must not be labelled submission-ready. When a major ambiguity prevents a clean rewrite, deliver the supported portion and identify exactly what remains unresolved.

### 6. Verify

Check negation, causal meaning, numbers, denominators, units, terminology, notation, equations, pseudocode, figure/table references, and agreement across title, abstract, Introduction, Methods, Results, and Conclusion.

Distinguish numerical improvement from statistical significance; iterations from evaluations and elapsed time; observed non-dominance from true Pareto optimality; repeatability from perturbation robustness; simulation from physical validation; empirical convergence from formal proof.

Retrieve authoritative sources when bibliographic support or venue rules must be checked and are unavailable. Report what was actually inspected. Wording checks do not establish literature completeness, scientific validity, or model-behavior evaluation.

## Return an actionable deliverable

For full revisions return: a short overall diagnosis; prioritized issues with severity, location, impact, action, evidence, and confidence from 0.00 to 1.00; complete revised passages; a claim-evidence matrix when useful; outstanding work; and a verification note. Confidence describes the editorial judgment, not the statistical probability of the scientific claim.

For targeted revisions, return the revised passage first, followed only by material changes and unresolved scientific ambiguities. Do not append every worksheet or checklist. Keep reviewer-response formatting specific to the supplied review and venue.

The author retains final scientific judgment. This Skill improves scientific communication; it does not replace missing research with stronger rhetoric.
